FPS Calculadora · com.fps.calculadora

Last updated: August 27, 2026

In short: FPS Calculadora collects no personal data, transmits nothing and shares nothing. All calculation happens on your device. The app uses the internet for one thing only: downloading the latest version of the public catalog of parts, games and prices. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

FPS Calculadora is a free, independently developed Android app that estimates the gaming performance (FPS) of a PC from the hardware combination the user picks. This document explains how the app handles information.

2. Personal data collected: none

The app does not collect and does not send any information to the developer or to third parties. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • name, email, phone number or any sign-up data — there is no login and no user account;
  • location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera;
  • advertising identifiers, IMEI, MAC address or phone number;
  • usage data, screen events, crash reports or performance metrics.

No advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK is bundled in the app.

3. Android permissions

The app declares two permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml, both serving the same purpose — keeping the catalog current:

  • INTERNET — to download the latest version of the catalog of parts, games and prices, published by the project itself at yuumi-32.github.io/FpsCalculadora;
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check whether the device is connected, so the download is not attempted pointlessly.

No other permission is requested: no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no access to your files.

What leaves your device in that download: nothing of yours. It is a request for a public, static file — the same one your browser would make when opening a page. No name, no identifier, none of your builds and no parameter about you travels with it. As with any internet access, the server hosting the file (GitHub Pages) records the IP address the request came from; the app sends nothing beyond that, and the developer has no access to those logs.

The calculation, the game database and the hardware catalog still ship inside the app package — it works fully without internet. The download merely replaces that data with a newer version when one exists.

4. Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores some information locally, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves the device:

  • Saved builds — the CPU, GPU, motherboard, memory and graphics settings you chose to save, with the nickname you gave them;
  • Calculator preferences — the last resolution, monitor refresh rate, graphics preset, Ray Tracing, Frame Generation and upscaling you selected;
  • An "onboarding seen" flag, so the welcome screen does not show up again.

None of this identifies you personally: it is only hardware and configuration choices.

5. Android Backup

The app allows Android Auto Backup (allowBackup). If Google Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may copy the data described in section 4 to your own Google Drive so it comes back when you switch devices. That backup is performed by Android and controlled by you in your device settings and Google Account; the app developer has no access to it. You can turn backup off under Settings → Google → Backup.

6. Export, import and sharing

  • Build code — the History screen lets you copy your builds as a text string and paste it back on another device. The code goes to the device clipboard; the app sends it to no server. What you do with that text afterwards is your choice.
  • Download result PNG — the app renders an image of the result and saves it to the device photo gallery (Pictures → FPS Calculadora) through the Android media API. The image stays on your device. This feature requires Android 10 or newer.
  • External links — if the app opens a link, it is handed to the device browser. From there, the policies of that website and of your browser apply.

7. Sharing with third parties

No data is shared, sold or transferred to third parties, for a simple reason: the app collects no data. The only connection it makes is fetching a public catalog file, and that request carries nothing of yours.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them — nor from anyone else.

9. How to delete your data

Since everything stays on the device, you control the data directly:

  • delete individual builds on the History screen;
  • or clear everything at once under Android Settings → Apps → FPS Calculadora → Storage → Clear data;
  • uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.

There is no deletion request to send to the developer, because there is no data on our side.

10. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, isolated from other applications by Android itself. Nothing you store in the app travels over the network. The catalog download uses HTTPS exclusively: the app refuses cleartext connections, and its internal WebView is restricted to files that shipped in the package.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new date at the top of the page, and the change will be described in the Google Play release notes.
